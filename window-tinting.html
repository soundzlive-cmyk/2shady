Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does window tinting take?

Most passenger vehicles take 2–4 hours depending on the number of windows. We often offer same-day appointments.

Is window tinting street legal?

Yes — as long as you stay within your state's VLT limits. We'll advise you on the legal limits for Massachusetts and help you choose a compliant shade.

How do I care for new tint?

Avoid rolling windows down for 3–5 days after installation to let the film fully cure. Clean with ammonia-free products only.

Will tint interfere with my GPS or phone signal?

Our ceramic films are non-metallic and 100% signal-safe. Dye and carbon films are also safe. Only very old metallic films can cause interference.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes — all our window film installations come with a lifetime warranty covering bubbling, peeling, and color change.
